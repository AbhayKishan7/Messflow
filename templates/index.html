Core Problems

Three Friction Points We Fix

Institutional mess management has long suffered from the same fundamental challenges.

Inaccurate Quantity Prediction

Kitchens cook blind — they don't know how many students will show up or what they'll want to eat, leading to massive overproduction or shortages.

Solved by pre-meal RSVP + AI forecasting

No Insight Into Food Preferences

Traditional feedback is biased and sparse. Kitchens have no reliable way to know which items students truly dislike until it's already wasted.

Solved by AI plate scanning analytics

Inefficient Surplus Handling

Surplus food is often discarded because there's no fast, reliable system to route it to those who need it before it spoils.

Solved by automated NGO routing alerts
How It Works

Four Steps to Zero Waste

A closed-loop system where every meal teaches the system to do better next time.

01

Students RSVP Meals

Students select their preferred dish and portion size before each meal. No more guessing for the kitchen.

02

AI Forecasts Demand

The system cross-references RSVPs with historical patterns to generate accurate cooking quantities per meal.

03

Plate Scanners Track Waste

AI cameras at dumping stations scan every plate, logging exactly what percentage of each item is being wasted.

04

Menu Updates + Surplus Routed

Every Monday, menus are revised based on waste data. Any unavoidable surplus is auto-dispatched to registered NGOs.
